COPPA Compliance Policy UNDER 13

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) is a United States federal law that restricts the collection of personal information from children under the age of 13. This policy describes how Proton# Studio complies with COPPA.

⚠️ Important — Parents Please Read

If your child is under 13 and wishes to use Proton# Studio, you must provide verified parental consent before an account can be created. Without this, children under 13 may use the software and website in read-only mode only — no accounts, no Marketplace, no Guides posting.

1. Age Requirements by Feature

2. Parental Consent Process

For users who indicate they are under 13 during account registration, the following process applies:

  1. The child's registration is paused and no data is stored beyond a temporary session
  2. A parental consent request is sent to the parent email address provided
  3. The parent must verify their identity and provide explicit written consent via a signed consent form
  4. Until consent is received and verified, no account is created and no personal data is retained
  5. If consent is not received within 14 days, all temporary data is permanently deleted

Parents may revoke consent at any time by contacting [email protected]. Upon revocation, the child's account and all associated data will be deleted within 5 business days.

3. What We Do NOT Collect From Children

For users under 13, even with parental consent, we do not collect:

  • Full name or home address
  • Phone number
  • Location data
  • Photos or video
  • Any information beyond what is strictly necessary for the account to function

Children under 13 with parental consent may create an account with only a username, parent-verified email, and date of birth. They may access the Marketplace in read-only mode. They may not post or comment in Guides, purchase Shards, or upload content.

4. Parents: Reviewing and Removing Your Child's Data

At any time, the parent or legal guardian of a child under 13 may:

  • Request to review all personal information we have collected from their child
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of their child's account and all associated data
  • Refuse to permit further collection or use of their child's information

To make any of these requests, email [email protected] with the subject line "COPPA Request — Child Account". Please include the child's username. We will respond within 5 business days and complete all deletions within 10 business days.

EDU Accounts and Schools

Schools using Proton# EDU may act as the consent authority for students under 13 under the school's existing parental consent agreements, provided the school has obtained appropriate parental permission as part of their technology use policies. Schools must contact us at [email protected] to establish an EDU agreement before student accounts are created.
